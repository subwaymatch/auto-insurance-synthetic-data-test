Lakefront: logo candidates

Every wordmark is Inter 4 converted to outlines, so the SVG files need no font. Lockups use Inter Display SemiBold at -0.04em. Icons are shown at 64, 32 and 16 px.

Candidates

01

Wordmark

Type only: Inter Display SemiBold at -0.04em. Nothing to explain, works at any size, and you can set it as live text in HTML. The icon is its L.

02

Reflection

Four bars read as a chart and as a skyline, mirrored in the lake below. Synthetic data as a reflection of a real city.

03

South-west corner

An L that holds the water in its corner. Chicago sits at the south-west corner of Lake Michigan, so the shape is also the map.

header size
04

Shoreline grid

City blocks on a street grid (or the cells of a table) against one unbroken lake. The shore steps south-east the way Chicago's does.

header size
05

Sunrise o

The o becomes the sun rising over the lake (sunrise is over the water in Chicago), its lower half reflected in bands. The mark lives inside the word.

06

Shield

The insurer's shield with a wave-cut horizon. The most literal of the six: it says insurance first and lake second.
