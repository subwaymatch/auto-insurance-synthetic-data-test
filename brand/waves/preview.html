Variations

A

Three waves

The logo's three bands, each turned into a ribbon of the same thickness that rises and falls in step with the others: about one and a half waves across the o.

header size
B

Two waves

Two broad ribbons with deeper, longer waves. The boldest, and the one most likely to keep its waves at small sizes.

header size
C

Staggered waves

Three ribbons, the middle one half a wave out of step, so crests sit over troughs like ripples on open water.

header size
D

Waves in perspective

Small, short waves near the horizon growing larger and longer towards the bottom, as waves look from the shore.

header size
E

One wave

A solid lake crossed by a single wave line: the straight horizon above it is kept, so the sun still reads as rising.

header size
F

Wave-topped lake

No bands: one solid lake whose surface is a wave. The simplest, and the clearest at 16 px.
